# The Monty Hall Problem

> *"Actually I'm an overnight success, but it took twenty years."*
> -Monty Hall

The Monty Hall problem is a statistics problem about chances changing as known information changes. You are given three doors, one has a car, 
while the others have goats.
You must select one, and then one of the two remaining doors is revealed to have a goat behind it. 
Then, again you are given an option, either switch to the remaining door or stay on your current one.

## Highlights
This app is designed to let you play the Monty Hall problem, or test the statistics of it.
To let you tackle it there are two options:

- Single: You can just play as normal and see how you do.
- Runner: Instead of you playing 1000 times to gain some data try using a runner, which will use random number generation to determine its selections. You can set how many times it plays and how often it switches. Statistics will be provided about its performance.

## Does this Sound Like a Game Show?

You may be wondering why this sounds like a game show, and that's because it is. Monty Hall is not a mathamatician and instead hosted a game show called 
*Let's Make a Deal* from 1963 to 1986, which used a similar situation, with some twists to keep the show entertaining and less weighted towards the contestants.
In 1975 this problem was named after Monty Hall by statistician Steven Selvin.

## Why did I make this?

> **TL;DR:** I love math and statistics and is bored in their dorm.

The Monty Hall problem is one that seems to always confuse people, they can't understand how its not just a 50/50 once a door is revealed.
This application was made because I wanted to have an application that could show the actual numbers to people.
Additionally, I did not want to make a calculator app as that seemed to generic.
